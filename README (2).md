## 🇬🇧 English

A legislative watch on corporate sustainability and due diligence in the European Union. I track the main directives and regulations from proposal through trilogue negotiations to transposition in Member States, focusing on the obligations they create for companies, suppliers, and supply chains.

### Index

**EU Framework** — [`/eu-framework`](./eu-framework)
- CSDDD (Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive)
- CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive)
- EU Taxonomy
- ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards)

**Member State Transposition** — [`/member-states`](./member-states)
- Spain
- France
- Germany
- Italy

**Impact Notes** — [`/impact-notes`](./impact-notes)
Short analyses broken down by actor type: company, supplier, supply chain.

**Coming soon**
- Relevant case law (`/case-law`)
- Interpretive guidance from the European Commission, EFRAG, and national supervisors (`/guidance`)
- Legislative timelines (`/timeline`)

### How each file is organised

Every entry follows a fixed template, see [`TEMPLATE-ENTRADA.md`](./TEMPLATE-ENTRADA.md), and is revised whenever the legislative process moves to a new stage. The last-updated date appears at the end of each entry.

### About this project

This repository complements [EU Policy Watch](https://stalaveralodos-ux.github.io/), extending its scope to corporate sustainability and business due diligence. It is designed as a quick-reference source for compliance, public affairs, and sustainability professionals.
